---
name: Plan
description: Produce a plan, change nothing
---

Produce a written plan and stop. Do not edit files, run commands that change
state, or begin the implementation — the user reviews the plan and decides
whether to proceed.

Decompose the work into steps that can each be verified on their own, and call
out anything that is genuinely uncertain rather than planning around it as if it
were known. Flag the risky step: the one where being wrong is expensive.
